Privacy

Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

The HiNoterBook is the business behind this website and the pre-order offer. You can reach us at hello@hinoterbook.example or on WhatsApp at +27 XX XXX XXXX.

What we collect

When you send a pre-order or a question, we receive the details you choose to share: your name, email address, mobile number, colour choice, province and any note you add.

This website does not ask for card or banking details, and it does not use advertising trackers.

When you send a pre-order, your details are emailed to our team and you receive a confirmation email. We use Resend, an email delivery service, to send these emails. Resend handles your details only to deliver them.

Why we use it

  • To contact you about your pre-order and confirm colour, delivery and payment.
  • To answer questions you send us.
  • To apply a reward you have revealed on the website.

We will not sell your information or use it for anything you haven't agreed to.

Your consent

We only use your details after you tick the consent box on our pre-order form, or when you contact us yourself. You can withdraw consent at any time by telling us.

Your rights under POPIA

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. You can also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as we need them to handle your pre-order and any follow-up, or as the law requires.

Meetings and recordings

When the assistant launches, captured meetings will contain other people's personal information. Always tell people when a meeting is being captured. We will publish full details on how recordings are stored and deleted before the assistant is available.

Small details stored on your device

If you reveal the scratch-card reward, your browser remembers it so it can be added to your pre-order form. This stays on your device and is not sent to us until you send your pre-order.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
