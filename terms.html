Pre-order terms

The simple rules behind the pre-order, in plain English.

The pre-order

The recommended retail price (RRP) is R7 499, once-off, and includes free HiNoter Earpods worth R999. Sending a pre-order reserves your place. It is not a payment and it is not a binding purchase until our team has confirmed the details with you and you have paid.

What happens next

Our team contacts you to confirm your colour, delivery and how to pay. If anything about the product or timing changes, we'll tell you before you pay.

The 10% reward

The scratch-card reward is 10% off your pre-order, so you pay R6 749 instead of R7 499. This is the maximum discount; it cannot be combined with other discounts. One reward per customer. Our team confirms the final amount with you before payment.

Changing your mind

You can cancel a pre-order at any time before you pay, by WhatsApp or email. After payment, the Consumer Protection Act and your other legal rights still apply.

The AI assistant

Features and integrations described on this website are planned and may change. We mark integrations as live only when they work.
