How it works

Before, during and after every meeting

Three simple stages. You stay in the conversation; your notes arrive in your inbox afterwards.

Before the meeting

Connect and prepare

Link your calendar and pick the meetings to capture.

  • Pick meetings from your calendar
  • See the agenda and last meeting's actions
  • Tell attendees the meeting will be captured
During the meeting

Listen, don't type

The listening device built into the folio captures the meeting and the assistant writes as people talk.

  • Live transcript with speaker names
  • Decisions, actions and risks marked as they happen
  • Your handwritten notes stay in the folio
After the meeting

Review from your inbox

When the call ends, every note is sent straight to your inbox. Review it, change anything, then share it.

  • Summary and full notes emailed to you
  • Action list with owners and dates
  • Follow-up email drafted for you

Notes you can use the minute the call ends

When the meeting ends, every note is sent straight to your inbox for you to review: a short summary, who does what, and a follow-up email ready to check and send. Here is an example.

In your inbox · Notes: Launch check-inExample

Launch check-in

Thandi N., Pieter V., Aisha K.

In one line

The supplier is two weeks late, so the launch moves from February to 3 March.

Decisions

  • New launch date: 3 March.
  • Keep the current price for the first 100 orders.

Still open

  • Who signs the supplier contract: Thandi to confirm.

Action items

3 people · 4 actions

  • Update the pricing sheet for the new datePieter · Fri
  • Tell the two launch partners about the changeAisha · Wed
  • Confirm who signs the supplier contractThandi · Thu
  • Book the next check-in for after the supplier callThandi
ToPieter, AishaSubjectLaunch moves to 3 March: next steps

Hi both,

Thanks for today. Quick recap: the supplier needs two more weeks, so we're launching on 3 March. We keep the current price for the first 100 orders.

Pieter, please update the pricing sheet by Friday. Aisha, could you let the two launch partners know by Wednesday? I'll confirm who signs the supplier contract.

Thandi
